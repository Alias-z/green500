Climate targets and known gaps

The public SBTi Target Dashboard matches Microsoft unambiguously as SBTi ID 40005011, ISIN US5949181045, and LEI INR2EJN1ERAN0W5ZP974. Its company file lists near-term Targets set, classified 1.5°C. Target records published 23 September 2019 say Microsoft will continue sourcing 100% renewable electricity annually through 2030 and reduce Scope 3 emissions intensity per unit of revenue 30% by 2030 from a 2017 base year while avoiding growth in absolute Scope 3 emissions. The same source lists Microsoft's net-zero commitment as Commitment removed, with reason Expired commitment; it must not be shown as a validated net-zero target.

The financial and environmental periods differ by one fiscal year. The environmental figures are company assertions, even where assurance material accompanies the report. This example has no independent, comparable social-outcome assessment, OSHA parent-company aggregation, market price, analyst estimate, or scenario-adjusted valuation. Those gaps remain explicit.
